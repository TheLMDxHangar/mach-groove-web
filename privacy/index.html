Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: SEPTEMBER 11, 2026 · LAST UPDATED: SEPTEMBER 25, 2026

Mach Groove is an iOS app by LMDx Tech that moves playlists between Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, and YouTube — as songs, music videos, or live performances. Same playlist, different vibe. This policy says plainly what we collect, where it lives, how long we keep it, and how to delete it. The short version: we collect almost nothing, we track nothing, and we never sell anything.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we collect exactly two things:

  • Your email address — so your Mach Groove account exists and we can tell it apart from everyone else's.
  • A user ID (a random identifier created for your account when you sign in) — how the app proves to our servers that a request is really yours.

That's the whole list. Your playlists and transfers stay on your phone — we don't keep a copy of what you transferred or what's in it.

What we never collect

  • No tracking. No analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no fingerprinting. The App Store "Do you track users?" answer is No, and it's true.
  • No listening surveillance. We see the playlists you choose to transfer, only while you transfer them. We don't monitor what you play.
  • No audio. Mach Groove moves playlist information (titles, artists, catalog IDs) — it never copies, downloads, or streams music.
  • No sale of data. Not to anyone, not ever. No ads, no data brokers.

Where your data lives

  • On your device: the sign-in tokens for your connected music services (in the iOS Keychain), your transfer history, lifetime stats, transfer logs, and preferences like theme. None of this leaves your phone unless you choose to send a log to support.
  • On our servers: your email and user ID, and nothing else.
  • In transit only: connecting Spotify routes its sign-in exchange through our server so secrets never ship inside the app — the token passes through and is not stored there.
  • Not personal data: the app reports anonymous song-catalog facts (for example, "this Spotify track ID and this Apple Music ID are the same recording") to make everyone's matches faster. These are facts about songs, not about you.

Your music-service connections

  • Spotify: authorized via Spotify's own sign-in; the token stays in your device Keychain. Revoke anytime at spotify.com → Account → Apps.
  • Apple Music: authorized through iOS itself (like Camera or Location) — we never hold a credential. Revoke in iOS Settings.
  • YouTube Music: authorized via Google Sign-In on your device. Disconnect anytime in the app.

Disconnecting a service in the app (or revoking it at the service) cuts Mach Groove's access immediately.

YouTube API Services

Mach Groove uses YouTube API Services. By using the YouTube features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Mach Groove caches YouTube API data (like search results) for no more than 30 days, keyed to songs — not to you. You can revoke Mach Groove's access to your Google/YouTube data at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How long we keep it

  • Email + user ID: as long as your account exists; deleted when your account is deleted.
  • Everything on your device: yours — deleting the app deletes it.
  • Cached catalog data: song facts only, no personal data; YouTube-sourced cache entries expire within 30 days.

Deleting your account and data

Delete your account anytime in the app: Profile → Delete Account. That deletes your account record (email, user ID) and everything tied to it on our servers, removes Mach Groove's access to your Apple or Google sign-in, and clears the app's data from your phone. Playlists you already created in Apple Music or YouTube stay in those apps.

Can't get into the app? Email support@mach-groove.app from the address you signed in with and we'll delete your account and confirm when it's done. Deleting the app from your phone also removes everything stored on the device.

Launch alerts (this website)

If you sign up for launch alerts on mach-groove.app, we store the email address you give us and which platforms you asked about (iOS/Android), solely to send you launch news. Stored on our own infrastructure, never shared or sold, deleted after the launch announcements wrap or anytime you ask at support@mach-groove.app.

This website

mach-groove.app is a static site; our hosting and font providers may log standard request data (like IP addresses) to serve it. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Children

Mach Groove is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with an updated date. Material changes to what we collect will be called out in the app.
